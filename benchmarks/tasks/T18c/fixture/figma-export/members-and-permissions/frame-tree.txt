    FRAME "Best practices" 300x587
      TEXT "BEST PRACTICES"
      TEXT "• A Table of members: person (Avatar, name, email), Role, Status Badge and a row-actions menu."
      TEXT "• Change a role in place with a Select of 2 to 4 roles. It saves at once and a Toast confirms; the invite dialog explains each role."
      TEXT "• Invite is the one primary Button in the header. It opens a Dialog with an email Field and a role Select."
      TEXT "• A pending invite shows a Pending Badge and offers Resend and Revoke instead of a role change."
      TEXT "• Removing a member opens a destructive AlertDialog that names the person and what they lose."
      TEXT "• The last Owner cannot be demoted or removed. The controls are disabled and the reason is written next to them in an Alert."
      TEXT "• The current user's row says You. Leaving the workspace is a separate action."
    FRAME "Anti-patterns" 300x347
      TEXT "ANTI-PATTERNS"
      TEXT "• Removing a member without naming them and the consequence."
      TEXT "• A disabled control with no stated reason."
      TEXT "• Role as free text instead of a Select of fixed roles."
      TEXT "• Letting the last Owner be removed."
      TEXT "• A permissions matrix of many checkboxes inside each row."
      TEXT "• Changing someone's role with no confirmation that it saved."
